中文 · CHINESE TRANSLATION
Fixed partitioning, dynamic partitioning, simple paging and simple segmentation are the main techniques.
固定分区、动态分区、简单分页和简单分区是主要技术。
Paging and segmentation use logical addresses and address translation.
分页和分区使用逻辑地址和地址翻译。
Each technique differs in fragmentation and in the size of the address space.
每种技术在碎片化和地址空间大小上都不同。

Model answers / 参考答案
  1. Fixed partitioning, dynamic partitioning, simple paging and simple segmentation are the main techniques.固定分区、动态分区、简单分页和简单分区是主要技术。
  2. Paging and segmentation use logical addresses and address translation.分页和分区使用逻辑地址和地址翻译。
  3. Each technique differs in fragmentation and in the size of the address space.每种技术在碎片化和地址空间大小上都不同。
