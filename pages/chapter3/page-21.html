Suspended Processes

挂起进程

Excerpted from “Ch03v2a.pdf” — Page 21
← Back Next →

Original Content (verbatim)

  • Swapping: involves moving part or all of a process from main memory to disk交换（Swapping）：把进程的部分或全部从主存移到磁盘
  • When none of the processes in main memory is in the Ready state, the OS swaps one of the blocked processes out onto disk into a suspend queue当主存中没有任何进程处于就绪态时，操作系统把某个被阻塞的进程换出到磁盘上的挂起队列
  • This is a queue of existing processes that have been temporarily kicked out of main memory, or suspended这是一条存放被暂时清出主存（即被挂起）的进程的队列
  • The OS then brings in another process from the suspend queue or it honors a new-process request操作系统随后从挂起队列中调入另一个进程，或满足新建进程的请求
  • Execution then continues with the newly arrived process随后继续执行新到达的进程
  • Swapping, however, is an I/O operation and therefore there is the potential for making the problem worse, not better. Because disk I/O is generally the fastest I/O on a system, swapping will usually enhance performance但交换是一种 I/O 操作，因此有可能使问题更糟而不是更好。由于磁盘 I/O 通常是系统中最快的 I/O，交换一般还是能提升性能
