Original Content (verbatim)

  • Processes have two characteristics: Resource Ownership and Scheduling/Execution.进程有两个特征：资源拥有（Resource Ownership）与调度/执行（Scheduling/Execution）。
  • Resource Ownership: a process includes a virtual address space to hold the process image资源拥有：进程包含一个虚拟地址空间，用来保存进程映像
  • The OS performs a protection function to prevent unwanted interference between processes with respect to resources操作系统执行保护功能，防止进程之间在资源方面发生不希望的相互干扰
  • Scheduling/Execution: follows an execution path that may be interleaved with other processes调度/执行：进程沿一条执行路径推进，该路径可能与其他进程交错
  • A process has an execution state (Running, Ready, etc.) and a dispatching priority, and is the entity that is scheduled and dispatched by the OS进程具有执行状态（运行、就绪等）和调度优先级，并且是被操作系统调度和分派的实体

ATwo concepts两个概念

  1. resource ownership: a virtual address space holds the process image资源拥有：保存进程映像的虚拟地址空间
  2. scheduling and execution: an execution path interleaved with other processes调度执行：可与其它进程交错的执行路径

BRole of the OS操作系统的角色

  1. performs a protection function执行保护功能
  2. prevents unwanted interference between processes防止进程间相互干扰
