User-Level Threads (ULTs)

用户级线程（ULT）

Excerpted from “Ch04v2a.pdf” — Page 17
← Back Next →

Original Content (verbatim)

  • All thread management is done by the application所有线程管理工作都由应用程序完成
  • The threads library creates, destroys and schedules threads线程库负责创建、销毁和调度线程
  • The kernel is not aware of the existence of threads内核并不知道线程的存在
  • The kernel schedules the process as a unit内核以进程为单位进行调度
