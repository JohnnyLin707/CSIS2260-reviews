Thread Synchronization

线程同步

Excerpted from “Ch04v2a.pdf” — Page 15

Original Content (verbatim)

  • It is necessary to synchronize the activities of the various threads必须同步各个线程的活动
  • All threads of a process share the same address space and other resources一个进程的所有线程共享同一地址空间和其他资源
  • Any alteration of a resource by one thread affects the other threads in the same process任一线程对资源的修改都会影响同一进程中的其他线程
