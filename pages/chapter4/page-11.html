Original Content (verbatim)

  • Most of the state information dealing with execution is maintained in thread-level data structures与执行有关的大部分状态信息都保存在线程级数据结构中
  • In an OS that supports threads, scheduling and dispatching is done on a thread basis在支持线程的操作系统中，调度与分派以线程为单位进行
  • Suspending a process involves suspending all threads of the process挂起一个进程意味着挂起该进程的所有线程
  • Termination of a process terminates all threads within the process进程终止时，其中的所有线程也一起终止
